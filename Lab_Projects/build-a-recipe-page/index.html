Pastel Vanilla Dream Cake

This soft and fluffy vanilla cake is layered with pastel cream and finished with a smooth, dreamy glaze. Perfect for any elegant occasion.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 cups sugar
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1 tbsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F) and grease your cake pans.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
  3. Add the eggs one at a time, then mix in vanilla.
  4. Alternate adding flour and milk until fully incorporated.
  5. Pour batter into pans and bake for 30–35 minutes.
  6. Let cakes cool, then layer with pastel-colored cream.
